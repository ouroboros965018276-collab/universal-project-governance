both tasks passed, all four positive checks passed, and all 11 executed broker

remains `UNASSESSED`, and this result authorizes no promotion.

## Interpretation and decision

The observed treatment totals were **9.8860% lower in tokens** and **33.5994%
lower in full callback time**; CLI time was 33.3514% lower. These are descriptive
one-pair development results. Resampling a single pair yields degenerate
intervals and cannot establish useful family-level uncertainty. The coverage
gate correctly fails even though the other stored gates pass. No cross-model,
cross-provider or independent efficiency claim follows from this probe.

Both conditions used the same current tool adapter, with exact runtime Skill
and planner text prepared before the measured calls. The comparison therefore
measures guidance under this adapter. The full callback includes per-dispatch
preparation/verification, CLI execution, grading and evidence commit, but excludes
runtime compilation/planner generation and initial client capability setup.
**Native preparation-cache and client-reuse benefits were not measured.** This
probe cannot reverse the earlier RC18 callback-latency finding or establish an
RC18-versus-RC19 end-to-end adapter improvement. Such a claim requires a separate
frozen matched comparison of the actual historical and current callbacks on
fresh tasks, including their real cold and warm costs. Existing quality and net
criteria remain unchanged.
